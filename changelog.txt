Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-10-01
  Features:
    - New purple Lightning group: lightning rods and lightning collectors get power (buffer capacity and discharge rate, i.e. how much strike energy can be stored and how fast it feeds the grid), recipe cost, item weight and attract range multipliers. Attract range extends how far lightning is attracted from.
    - Per-building item weight multipliers (rocket lift capacity scales with item weight).
    - Heating radius multipliers for heat pipes, nuclear reactors and heating towers (ambient heat thaws frozen terrain/entities, e.g. Aquilo; reactors have no vanilla radius, so values above 1 enable it).
    - Nuclear reactor neighbour bonus toggle (default on, vanilla behaviour) and heating tower neighbour bonus toggle (default off, vanilla behaviour).
    - New "Boiler" group entries: the vanilla coal boiler now has fuel consumption, recipe cost and item weight multipliers.
    - Optional compatibility pass (off by default) that applies the multipliers to buildings added by other mods. Modded buildings inherit the multipliers of the vanilla building they resemble: reactors burning nuclear fuel use the nuclear reactor multipliers, other reactors use the heating tower ones; boilers burning fuel use the boiler multipliers, heat-sourced boilers use the heat exchanger ones; generators with fluid at 400 degrees or hotter use the steam turbine multipliers, cooler ones the steam engine ones; fusion generators burning a fluid other than fusion plasma (e.g. Muluna's condenser turbine) are treated as steam turbines; other prototype types are matched by name (e.g. Muluna's vacuum heating tower, which inherits the heating tower multipliers). Classified buildings get a colour-coded tooltip naming the vanilla building whose multipliers they inherit, and a manual override list can exclude specific buildings (e.g. Muluna's vacuum boiler, a crafting machine).
  Changes:
    - Heat pipe heating radius multiplier is now generated by the shared building loop (same behaviour).

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-01
  Features:
    - Initial release: startup settings for per-building power production multipliers for solar panels, accumulators, nuclear reactors, heating towers, heat interfaces, heat exchangers, steam engines, steam turbines, fusion reactors and fusion generators.
    - Matching per-building recipe cost multipliers (heat pipes get a recipe multiplier only). Recipe costs are never scaled below 1.
    - Settings are grouped and color-coded in their names: yellow for solar, green for nuclear/heat, orange for steam, blue for fusion.
